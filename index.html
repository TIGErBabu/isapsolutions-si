A Story, Not Another Card Grid

9:00 AM. An invoice is blocked. What happens next?

"Before, someone chased it for a week. Now, the system chases it — and a person still decides."

Illustrative scenario only — not a client case, no metrics claimed. It shows the same workflow as the SAP & Approach page, in plain language.

Before

An invoice doesn't match its purchase order. Accounts Payable emails procurement, procurement checks goods receipt, finance waits. The supplier calls. The payment slips. Nobody can see, in one place, what is actually missing.

After — with an agent, under approval

1. Trigger: the blocked invoice event starts the agent.
2. Retrieve: it pulls the PO, goods receipt and payment policy — scoped to what the approver could see.
3. Reason: it drafts what is wrong and what would fix it, with sources shown.
4. Approve: a human approves. Nothing posts on its own.
5. Audit: trigger, data, approver and result are logged.
