SAP TRACK

Modules & Conversion

BTP TRACK

Extensions & Integration

SAP Business Technology Platform: Integration Suite for SAP-to-non-SAP flows, Extension Suite / CAP for side-by-side extensions that keep the core clean, and Fiori for role-based user experience. Extensions live outside the core wherever possible.

DATA & AI TRACK

Data, AI & Migration

Migration Cockpit and data profiling/cleansing before cutover, master-data governance after it. SAP AI in SAP's own terms: Joule assistants, SAP AI Core, Datasphere for harmonized data, and Signavio for process analysis — used where they fit the workflow, not as buzzwords.

Brownfield reality — the hard part, named plainly

Synthetic test data is the easy part. The work that actually decides a migration is below, and it is priced and planned first, not discovered at cutover.

01

Custom Z-code

Legacy Z-tables, Z-programs and custom enhancements are inventoried, then remediated, retired, or rebuilt as clean BTP extensions. Unremediated custom code is the most common reason a brownfield cutover slips.

02

Master-data cleansing

Duplicate vendors, customers and materials are profiled, cleansed and governed before migration — not after go-live, when bad data becomes bad decisions.

03

Testing, downtime & rollback

Integration, user-acceptance and performance testing, a stated downtime window, and a rollback plan for cutover. If a step cannot be rolled back, it is not approved for cutover.

Cost & governance — before any agent posts anything

COST

BTP consumption control

BTP credits, API calls and AI usage are monitored with budgets and alerts, and agent designs are chosen partly on cost — not just capability. A design that cannot be run affordably is not a design.

APPROVAL

Human-in-the-loop

Agents draft, retrieve and recommend. A human approves before anything posts to finance, inventory or a customer record. No agent posts unsupervised to the core.

AUDIT

Audit trail & access

Every agent action is logged — who triggered it, what data it used, who approved it. Role-based access applies to agents exactly as it applies to people.

What is actually unified: SAP and Oracle keep their own cores. What is shared is the data layer, the agent approval/audit layer, and monitoring — that shared governance is the "Apex." It is not a claim that one ERP replaces the other.
